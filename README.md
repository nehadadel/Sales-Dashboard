# Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive sales analysis project developed to monitor business performance, evaluate profitability, and identify underperforming products.

## Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- Data Cleaning and Transformation
- Sales and Profit Analysis
- KPI Cards and Interactive Filters
- Year-over-Year (YoY) Sales Growth
- Running Total and Profit Margin

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Analysis
Provides an overview of sales and profitability through key performance indicators and visualizations, including:
- Total Sales and Total Profit
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Profit and Sales by Category
- Sales Distribution by Region
- Year, Region, City, and Profitability Category filters

### 2. Product Analysis
Focuses on product-level profitability, including:
- Bottom 5 Loss-Making Products
- Top 5 Least Profitable Products
- Product-level sales and profit indicators
- Filters for Region, Sub-Category, City, and Profitability Category

## Project Objective
To help business owners monitor sales performance, understand profitability, and identify products that may require further investigation.
